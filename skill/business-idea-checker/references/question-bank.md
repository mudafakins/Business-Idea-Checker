# Question bank

Fixed questions, asked in stage order. You may reword them to fit the idea, skip one that an
earlier answer clearly covers, and ask one follow-up when an answer is too vague to check.
Do not add new topics. "I don't know" is always allowed.

## Stage 0: Starting point
1. Where are you right now? One idea / a few ideas / no idea yet.
   (Or they paste everything at once: sort it into the questions below and ask only for gaps.)

## Stage 1: About you and your non-negotiables (everyone)
2. Which country and city will you run this from?
3. How much money can you put in without hurting yourself if it's lost?
4. How much time can you give it each week?
5. What are you good at, or have access to, that most people don't? (skills, contacts, an
   audience, a location)
6. When do you need it to start earning? From day 1 / within 6 months / 1 to 2 years / I can wait
7. What will you refuse to do? (for example: no inventory, no employees, no selling in
   person, no borrowing, no building new technology; plus their own rules)

## Stage 2: The idea
- One idea: 8. Describe it in one or two sentences.
- A few ideas: 8. List them, one per line. (Then research, rate and rank; they pick one.)
- No idea: 8. What problems annoy you or people around you? 9. Which industries or customers
  do you know well? (Then suggest five ideas; they pick one or ask for more.)

After this, decide the business type (product, service, software, local business or
marketplace) and fit the later wording to it.

## Stage 3: Customer and problem
10. Who exactly buys this? Describe one real person if you can.
11. What problem does it solve for them, and what do they do about it today?
12. Why would they switch to you?
13. Have you spoken to anyone who would buy it? No / a few people / many / I already have
    paying customers

## Stage 4: Market and competitors
14. Who do you think your competitors are?
15. Show the competitors you found, with prices. Which is closest to what you want to be?
16. What will you do that they don't?

## Stage 5: Money
17. What will you charge? (Show the competitor price range beside the question.)
18. How will customers find you and buy? (Suggest channels that fit their country and type.)
19. Will a customer buy once or keep coming back? How often?
20. What does it cost you to deliver one sale? (If unknown, estimate from research and tag it.)

## Batching in a chat
Good groupings: questions 2-4; 5-7; 8 (and 9); 10-13; 14 then 15-16 once research is in;
17-20. Five or six messages from them in all is a good pace.
