# Report template

One scrolling page, readable in about three minutes. Plain words. The verdict and scorecard
come first so the answer is visible straight away. Tag every factual number: "₹399 (verified)",
"about 8% a year (estimated)", "₹170 (assumed)". Targets you set are plans and take no tag.

```markdown
# [Idea in a few words]: [Verdict]

**Verdict: [Pursue / Test small first / Drop or rethink]** · [High/Medium/Low] confidence
([N]% of the evidence is verified)

[Two or three sentences on why, in plain words. If capped, say so.]

| Area | Rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | [Strong/Okay/Weak] | [one line with its main evidence] |
| Market | ... | ... |
| Competition | ... | ... |
| Money | ... | ... |
| Fit | ... | ... |
| Risk | ... | ... |

## Your idea in one sentence
[What it is, who it is for, why they would choose it.]
[If there is a better nearby version they haven't taken, or the verdict is Drop or rethink:]
**Nearest version that could work:** [one sentence] — [why, one sentence]

## Simplified business plan
**Customer:** [who buys, what problem it solves]
**Market:** [size and direction, tagged] 
| Competitor | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| [3 to 5 real competitors with prices and links] | | |
**Your edge:** [the gap you fill]. **Does not count as a difference:** [claims everyone makes].
**How you sell:** [3 to 5 channels, in priority order]
**Money:**
| | |
|---|---|
| Price per sale | ₹... (tag) |

| You keep per sale | ... |
| Cost to win a customer | ... |

| Sales needed to cover start-up costs | ... |
**Start-up budget:** [first-stage items and total, against their budget]

## Alerts
[Each clash found while checking: what they said · what the data says (with source) ·
suggested change. Mark serious ones.]

## What to do next
**Cheapest test:** [one experiment aimed at the riskiest assumption] · Budget: [...] ·
Time limit: [...]
**Green gates (keep going if):** [3 to 4 measures, each with a number and time window]
**Kill switch (stop if):** [2 to 3 measures with numbers]
**First 30 days:** [5 to 7 short, concrete steps]

## How sure we are
[N]% verified. Tags: verified = read on a source page; estimated = worked out, reasoning
shown; assumed = your own figure or no evidence.
**What would change the verdict:** [the one or two facts that would move it up or down]
**Sources:** [the fact list: id, finding, tag, link]

*This is guidance, not financial or legal advice.*
```
